PookieScroll ("the app", "we") is an iOS screen-time companion built on Apple's official Family Controls, DeviceActivity, and ManagedSettings frameworks. It runs entirely on your device. We do not operate any server, we do not create user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your screen-time data, app usage, or any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. PookieScroll has no backend service. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking pixel, and no third-party network code. The app never sends your usage data, your selected blocked apps, your goal, your mascot choice, or anything else, to us or any third party.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally in iOS-managed storage (a shared app group accessible only to PookieScroll and its extensions):

  • Your daily screen-time goal
  • Your block routines (start/end times, target apps or categories, active weekdays)
  • Your usage limits (per-app or per-category caps)
  • Quick-block selections
  • Your chosen mascot (kraken or ghost)
  • UI preferences (theme, language, app icon variant)
  • Cached screen-time totals (so the widget and mascot can react quickly)

Family Controls authorization

When you first open the app, iOS asks you to grant Family Controls (Screen Time) authorization. This authorization is managed by Apple. With it, iOS reports your screen-time totals to PookieScroll and lets PookieScroll apply the shields you configure. The reports remain on your device. You can revoke this authorization at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

Apple Family Activity Picker

When you select apps to block or limit, iOS presents Apple's FamilyActivityPicker. Your selections are stored on your device as opaque tokens; PookieScroll does not learn the human-readable identities of the apps you pick beyond what iOS allows it to display.

Analytics & tracking (the iOS app)

None. The PookieScroll iOS app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking code. It does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track you.

Analytics & tracking (this website)

This marketing website uses Google Analytics 4 (property G-FSJRFQ556N) to count anonymous visits and understand which pages people read. The iOS app itself contains zero analytics code; this section only applies to the website.

We run GA4 in cookieless mode: Google's Consent Mode v2 is configured with analytics_storage: 'denied' permanently. In practice this means:

  • No cookies are set in your browser — no _ga, no _ga_FSJRFQ556N, none of the usual Google Analytics cookies.
  • No persistent identifier (Client ID) is created for you. We can't recognise you across visits.
  • No localStorage / IndexedDB / device-fingerprint is written by GA4.
  • Google receives an anonymous, aggregated "ping" per page-view: page URL, referrer, browser language, approximate device class. Your IP address is anonymised on Google's servers before storage.

Because nothing is stored on or read from your device, ePrivacy Art. 5(3) (Austrian TKG § 165) does not require a consent banner for this use. If you would still rather have no analytics ping go out at all, install Google's official opt-out browser add-on, or use a tracker-blocking browser (Brave, Firefox Strict, Safari with Privacy Report) — all standard ad-blockers will also block googletagmanager.com.

Recipients & processors

If you tap ACCEPT in the cookie banner, pseudonymous analytics data is shared with the following processors (Art. 13(1)(e) GDPR):

  • Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) — EU-fronted data controller / joint controller for Google Analytics. Our Data Processing Addendum with Google is the one published at business.safety.google.
  • Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) — operates the Google Analytics backend. See "International transfers" below.
  • GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) — hosts this static website via GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive your IP address and User-Agent for the purpose of serving the page (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in operating the website).

International transfers (Schrems II)

Google LLC and GitHub, Inc. are US companies. Personal data (in our case: pseudonymous identifiers, IP addresses processed by GitHub at the moment of page-serve, browser metadata sent to Google Analytics if you accepted) may therefore be transferred to the United States.

The legal safeguards we rely on under Chapter V GDPR:

  • EU–US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) — Google LLC is DPF-certified (active certification, last verified before publication). Transfers to Google LLC therefore rely on an adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) — in addition to the DPF, Google's Data Processing Addendum incorporates the 2021 EU SCCs as a fallback under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR.
  • Pseudonymisation — we send no name, email, or other directly-identifying field to Google Analytics. Google's IP-anonymisation runs on Google's servers before storage.
  • GitHub Pages serves the static HTML and does not see your interactions inside the page; its processing is limited to log entries necessary to deliver the page.

If you would rather your data not leave the EU at all, click REJECT in the cookie banner. We will still be able to host the page (GitHub Pages is the host of record), but no analytics data will be transmitted.

Retention

  • Google Analytics 4 event data: 2 months (GA4 admin "Data retention" minimum, kept at default). Because no Client ID is set, GA4 cannot tie events together into a "user" anyway — the data is effectively aggregate from day one.
  • GitHub Pages access logs: 14 days, controlled by GitHub.
  • No cookies are set, so there is no cookie lifetime to disclose.

Legal basis (GDPR)

For visitors in the EU / EEA / UK we rely on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest for the cookieless analytics ping (understanding aggregate visitor counts to a small marketing site). The balancing-of-interests test under Art. 6(1)(f) is favourable here because: no persistent identifier is created, no cross-site tracking is possible, IP addresses are anonymised, and a straightforward opt-out exists (any tracker-blocker). The same basis applies to GitHub Pages serving the static HTML.

For the international transfers involved (Google LLC and GitHub Inc. in the US receive request metadata) we additionally rely on Art. 45 GDPR (EU–US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision) and Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR (Standard Contractual Clauses).

App Store & Apple

When you install PookieScroll from the App Store, Apple collects standard purchase, install, and (if you have opted in) crash information independently of us. We do not have access to your Apple ID or to App Store analytics that identify you. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Children

PookieScroll is rated 4+ on the App Store but is not directed at children. The Family Controls APIs the app relies on are Apple-managed and intended for users who can grant their own Screen Time access. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone — from the app or this website — under 14 years of age (Austria's digital-consent age under § 4(4) DSG 2018, which derogates downward from Art. 8(1) GDPR's default of 16).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor in your care has provided data via this website (e.g. accepted analytics cookies), email hi@pookiescroll.app and we will erase any associated GA pseudonymous identifier from our reports on request. Note that GA data on Google's side is fully aggregated after the 2-month retention window.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal information about you on our servers, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, export, or delete on request. To remove all PookieScroll data from your device, delete the app — iOS will purge its local storage.

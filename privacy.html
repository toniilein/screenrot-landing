Policy.

LAST UPDATED: 31 MAY 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lionscraft Pte. Ltd. (“Lionscraft”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with PookieScroll.

PookieScroll is designed to minimise data collection. We do not operate a backend server for user profiles, do not require user accounts, do not use analytics SDKs in the iOS app, do not show ads, and do not track users across apps or websites.

PookieScroll is provided by Lionscraft Pte. Ltd., a company incorporated in Singapore.

1. Summary

PookieScroll is an iOS app for digital wellbeing and screen time self-management.

In general:

  • Your screen time usage data is processed on your device.
  • Your screen time usage data is not sent to Lionscraft.
  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not ask for your email address, phone number, or name inside the app.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs in the iOS app.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs in the iOS app.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Limited subscription-related information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat to provide purchases and subscription entitlement status.
  • The PookieScroll website uses cookieless analytics, as described in Section 17.

4. Information Processed On Your Device

PookieScroll may process information locally on your device to provide app functionality, including:

  • screen time and device activity information made available through Apple’s Screen Time, DeviceActivity, and Family Controls frameworks;
  • your selected routines, app limits, and blocking preferences;
  • your Pookie Score or similar in-app status indicators;
  • your app settings;
  • locally generated mood cards or share images.

This information is used to operate the app on your device. It is stored on your device and/or in an Apple App Group container. It is not sent to Lionscraft.

You can revoke Screen Time / Family Controls permission at any time in iOS Settings.

PookieScroll does not use iCloud sync in this version.

5. Subscription and Purchase Information

PookieScroll uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases and RevenueCat to help manage subscription entitlement status.

Apple may process information associated with your Apple ID, payment method, App Store account, purchase history, tax, billing, and refund handling under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

RevenueCat may process limited subscription-related information for entitlement management, such as an anonymous app user identifier, purchase or subscription events, product or entitlement information, subscription status, device type, operating system, Apple receipt information, last-seen time, and limited technical metadata associated with normal HTTPS requests, such as IP address or locale information where technically received.

PookieScroll does not use an advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not use this information, or any other information, to track you across apps or websites. We do not use advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs in the iOS app.

RevenueCat does not process your payment card details for PookieScroll. Payments are processed by Apple.

12. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Because PookieScroll does not operate user accounts or a backend user profile system, most app settings and screen time-related data are stored locally on your device and can generally be removed by deleting the app, subject to iOS behaviour.

Subscription-related records may be retained by Apple, RevenueCat, or us for as long as needed to provide entitlement status, handle billing or refund issues, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain business records.

RevenueCat may retain subscription-related records according to its own retention schedule, including where needed for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, or service-operation purposes.

Website analytics information is used in aggregate form to understand website usage. GitHub Pages and Google may retain limited website request or measurement information according to their own service terms and retention practices.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain support records, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

13. Deletion

You may delete local app data by deleting PookieScroll from your device, subject to iOS behaviour.

Deleting the app does not cancel your Apple subscription. You must cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID account settings.

To request deletion of personal data that we control, contact us at contact@pookiescroll.app. Because we do not maintain user accounts, we may need limited information from you to identify relevant records, such as your support email address or information needed to locate subscription-related records.

Because PookieScroll does not use accounts, we may not be able to identify subscription-related records from your name or email address alone. We may ask you to provide information available in the app or from your Apple subscription record so that we can locate the relevant RevenueCat entitlement record.

For Apple purchase records, you may need to contact Apple directly. For RevenueCat entitlement data, we may coordinate with RevenueCat where we are able to identify the relevant anonymous user record.

You can prevent the website analytics signal by using a browser or browser extension that blocks Google Analytics.

17. Cookies and Tracking

The PookieScroll iOS app does not use cookies, tracking technologies, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs.

Our website at pookiescroll.app uses Google Analytics 4 in a cookieless configuration to measure aggregate visitor numbers. Google Consent Mode is configured so that analytics storage is denied. In this configuration, Google Analytics does not set analytics cookies, create a persistent identifier, or read information from your device for analytics storage.

Google may receive limited measurement information for each page view, such as the page URL, referrer, and general browser or device information. Google may also process IP address information as part of providing the analytics service. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process IP address and request information as needed to serve the website.

The iOS app contains no analytics code. The website analytics described above applies only to pookiescroll.app.

This website analytics configuration does not use advertising cookies or enable cross-site tracking. You can prevent the analytics signal entirely by using a tracker-blocking browser or browser extension that blocks Google Analytics.

If our website analytics configuration changes, for example if cookies, persistent identifiers, advertising technologies, contact forms, or marketing tools are introduced, we will update this Privacy Policy or provide a separate notice where required.

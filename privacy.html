What we collect

PookieScroll runs entirely on your device using Apple's Family Controls, DeviceActivity, and Managed Settings APIs. We do not operate any server, do not create user accounts, and do not transmit your screen-time data, app usage, or any personal information off your phone.

Data stored on your device

Your daily screen-time goal, block routines, usage limits, chosen mascot, and related preferences are stored locally in iOS-managed storage (a shared app group) and accessed only by the PookieScroll app, its widget, and its extensions on the same device.

Analytics & tracking

None. PookieScroll does not include any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (we don't track you).

Family Controls authorization

PookieScroll requests Family Controls (Screen Time) authorization so iOS can report your usage to the app and apply the shields you configure. This authorization is handled by Apple; PookieScroll receives only aggregated reports that stay on your device.

Apple's role

If you install via the App Store, Apple collects standard purchase, install, and crash information independently of PookieScroll. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Children

PookieScroll is not directed at children under 13. The Family Controls APIs the app uses are Apple-managed and intended for users who have granted Screen Time access on their own device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and post the revised version at this URL.
